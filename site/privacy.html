Privacy

What this site does with your information

The self-check

The self-check runs in your browser. Your answers are not sent anywhere and are not stored. When you close the page, they are gone.

The contact form

If you choose to send the contact form, the details you enter, and your self-check summary if you attached it, are sent to me. The form is handled by Netlify, the company that hosts this site.

I use what you send only to reply to you and to prepare for a conversation with you. I do not sell it, share it for marketing, or add you to a mailing list.

Your choices

You can ask me to delete what you sent at any time. Write to hanad.s@icloud.com.

Analytics

This site uses no tracking cookies and no advertising tools.
