# AI Governance Notes

Working notes on AI governance frameworks and how organizations actually
implement them, written from an operations perspective with a Canadian
and public-sector lens.

## Why this exists

I'm an applied AI student in Toronto with 15+ years of experience in
project coordination and operational governance. I'm building a
portfolio of practical governance work: reading the primary sources,
documenting what they require of an organization, and publishing the
results so others can use or challenge them.

## What's here

- `/frameworks/nist-ai-rmf/` - plain-language notes on the NIST AI Risk
  Management Framework, organized by function, with who owns each
  subcategory and what evidence would show it was done
- `/templates/` - AI tool registry and policy templates (coming)
- `/newsletter/` - drafts and issues (coming)
- `/site/` - source for my site, [answerable-ai.netlify.app](https://answerable-ai.netlify.app/)

## Status

Work in progress. Notes reflect my current understanding and will be
corrected as I read more. Corrections and disagreement are welcome;
open an issue.
